| New Article XI — Document Retention and Destruction | Form 990 Part VI asks whether you have one. Costs one resolution now; otherwise it's a "no" every year forever. |
| New Article XII — Whistleblower Protection | Same. Form 990 asks by name. |
| New Article XIII — Compensation of Officers and Key Persons | Same, and it sets up the IRS rebuttable presumption of reasonableness if you ever pay anyone. |
| New Article X — Field Operations and Permitting | **The one that actually matters.** The Corporation cannot hold a wildlife permit; only individuals can. This article makes that constraint a governance obligation instead of an undocumented dependency on one person. |
| Indemnification extended to permitted activity (XV) | Every relocation is legally an individual's permitted act performed on the Corporation's behalf. Generic indemnification language does not obviously reach that. |
| 4.2 related-party limit made operative | Your draft stated an intention. This states a rule. |

## ARTICLE I — NAME AND OFFICES

**1.1 Name.** The name of the corporation is Save Snakes, Inc. (the "Corporation").

**1.2 Principal Office.** The principal office of the Corporation is located in [City of Richmond], Virginia. The Corporation may have such other offices as the board of directors may determine.

**1.3 Registered Office and Agent.** The Corporation shall continuously maintain a registered office and a registered agent in the Commonwealth of Virginia as required by law.

## ARTICLE II — PURPOSE

The Corporation is organized and operated exclusively for charitable and educational purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, as set forth in its Articles of Incorporation, including the humane, no-kill rescue and relocation of snakes without charge; public education regarding native snakes and human-wildlife coexistence; and the recruitment, training, and sponsorship of individuals into the permits and certifications the Commonwealth requires for lawful wildlife relocation.

## ARTICLE III — NO MEMBERS

The Corporation shall have no members. All rights, powers, and authority that would otherwise vest in members are vested in the board of directors.

## ARTICLE IV — BOARD OF DIRECTORS

**4.1 General Powers.** The affairs of the Corporation shall be managed by, or under the direction of, its board of directors.

**4.2 Number and Composition.** The board shall consist of no fewer than three (3) and no more than [nine (9)] directors, the exact number to be fixed from time to time by resolution of the board. A majority of the directors then in office shall not be related to one another by blood, marriage, adoption, or outside business relationship, and shall not be compensated by the Corporation.

**4.3 Term.** Each director shall serve a term of [two (2)] years and may be re-elected. Terms may be staggered by board resolution.

**4.4 Election.** Directors shall be elected by the affirmative vote of a majority of the directors then in office.

**4.5 Vacancies.** A vacancy, including one resulting from an increase in the number of directors, may be filled by a majority of the directors then in office, whether or not such directors constitute a quorum.

**4.6 Removal.** A director may be removed, with or without cause, by the affirmative vote of a majority of the directors then in office, excluding the director whose removal is under consideration.

**4.7 Resignation.** A director may resign at any time by written notice to the President or the Secretary.

**4.8 Compensation.** Directors shall serve without compensation for service as directors, but may be reimbursed for reasonable expenses actually incurred. A director may be paid reasonable compensation for distinct services actually rendered to the Corporation only in accordance with Article XIII and the Conflict of Interest Policy.

**4.9 Advisory Bodies.** The board may establish one or more advisory bodies, including a youth or student advisory body, whose members are not directors, do not vote, do not hold fiduciary duties to the Corporation, and serve at the pleasure of the board. Participation on an advisory body does not confer any authority to act on behalf of the Corporation.

## ARTICLE V — MEETINGS OF THE BOARD

**5.1 Regular Meetings.** The board shall hold no fewer than [four (4)] regular meetings each year.

**5.2 Annual Meeting.** An annual meeting shall be held to elect officers, review the annual financial report, and conduct such other business as may come before it.

**5.3 Special Meetings.** Special meetings may be called by the President or by any [two (2)] directors.

**5.4 Notice.** Notice of the time, date, and place or means of each meeting shall be given at least [five (5)] days in advance by email, mail, or telephone. Notice may be waived in writing before or after the meeting, and attendance constitutes waiver except where a director attends for the express purpose of objecting to the transaction of business.

**5.5 Quorum.** A majority of the directors then in office shall constitute a quorum.

**5.6 Voting.** The act of a majority of the directors present at a meeting at which a quorum is present shall be the act of the board, except where a greater vote is required by law, the Articles, or these Bylaws.

**5.7 Electronic Participation.** Directors may participate by conference telephone or video conference by means of which all persons participating can simultaneously hear one another. Such participation constitutes presence in person.

**5.8 Action Without a Meeting.** Any action required or permitted to be taken at a meeting may be taken without a meeting if all directors consent in writing, including by electronic mail. Such consents shall be filed with the minutes.

## ARTICLE VI — OFFICERS

**6.1 Officers.** The officers of the Corporation shall be a President, a Secretary, and a Treasurer, and such other officers as the board may elect. One person may hold more than one office, except that the same person shall not simultaneously hold the offices of President and Secretary.

**6.2 Election and Term.** Officers shall be elected by the board and shall serve [one (1)]-year terms or until a successor is elected.

**6.3 President.** Presides at meetings of the board and provides general supervision of the affairs of the Corporation.

**6.4 Secretary.** Keeps the minutes and records of the Corporation, gives required notices, and maintains the corporate records binder.

**6.5 Treasurer.** Has custody of the funds of the Corporation, keeps its financial records, presents financial reports to the board, and oversees required filings including the IRS Form 990 series, the SCC annual registration, and the VDACS charitable solicitation renewal.

**6.6 Removal and Vacancies.** Any officer may be removed by the board with or without cause. Vacancies shall be filled by the board.

## ARTICLE VII — COMMITTEES

The board may establish standing or special committees. Each committee shall consist of [two (2)] or more directors and shall act only within the authority expressly delegated to it by board resolution. No committee may take any action reserved to the board by law.

## ARTICLE VIII — CONFLICT OF INTEREST

The Corporation shall adopt and follow a written Conflict of Interest Policy. Each director, officer, and member of a committee with board-delegated powers shall sign the policy's annual disclosure statement upon election and annually thereafter. Signed statements shall be retained in the corporate records.

## ARTICLE IX — CONTRACTS, CHECKS, DEPOSITS, AND FUNDS

**9.1** The board may authorize any officer or agent to enter into contracts on behalf of the Corporation, and such authority may be general or confined to specific instances.

**9.2** Any check, withdrawal, or disbursement in excess of $[500] shall require either two (2) authorized signatures or prior board approval.

**9.3** All funds of the Corporation shall be deposited to the credit of the Corporation at such financial institutions as the board may designate.

**9.4** No director or officer shall use a personal account to receive or hold funds of the Corporation.

## ARTICLE X — FIELD OPERATIONS AND PERMITTING

**10.1 Compliance.** All wildlife capture, handling, transport, and release conducted by or on behalf of the Corporation shall comply with the permits, certifications, and conditions required by the Virginia Department of Wildlife Resources and any other applicable authority.

**10.2 Permits Held Individually.** The board acknowledges that the permits required for this work are issued to individuals and not to organizations, and that the Corporation therefore conducts field operations through individually permitted persons acting on its behalf.

**10.3 Verification.** The Secretary shall verify and record, no less than annually, the current permit and certification status of every person performing field operations on behalf of the Corporation. No person shall perform any activity requiring a permit on behalf of the Corporation without such verification on file.

**10.4 Operational Continuity.** The Corporation shall maintain no fewer than [two (2)] currently permitted operators on its active roster. The Corporation shall not publicly represent any guaranteed or time-bound response capability at any time when fewer than that number are available.

**10.5 Field Operations Policy.** The board shall adopt and maintain a written field operations policy governing supervision of unpermitted volunteers, permitted and prohibited volunteer activities, safety equipment, species restrictions, incident reporting, and the participation of minors. The policy shall be reviewed annually.

**10.6 Records.** Activity records required by any permit shall be maintained so as to satisfy the permit conditions, and copies shall be retained in the corporate records. Reports filed with the Virginia Department of Wildlife Resources shall be retained permanently.

**10.7 Incidents.** Any bite, envenomation, injury, property damage, or regulatory contact arising from field operations shall be reported to the President and the board promptly, and shall be recorded in the minutes of the next meeting.

## ARTICLE XI — DOCUMENT RETENTION AND DESTRUCTION

**11.1 Policy.** The Corporation shall retain records for the periods set forth below and shall destroy them thereafter in a secure manner, except that no record shall be destroyed if it is relevant to any pending or reasonably anticipated litigation, investigation, or audit.

| Record | Retention |
|---|---|
| Articles, Bylaws, and amendments | Permanent |
| Board and committee minutes | Permanent |
| IRS determination letter and Form 1023 | Permanent |
| Annual returns (Form 990 series) | Permanent |
| Permit records and DWR annual reports | Permanent |
| Conflict of interest disclosure statements | 7 years |
| Financial records, bank statements, receipts | 7 years |
| Insurance policies and claims | 7 years after expiration |
| Volunteer records, waivers, and incident reports | 7 years, or 7 years past the volunteer's 21st birthday if a minor, whichever is later |
| Donor records and acknowledgments | 7 years |
| Correspondence, general | 3 years |

**11.2 Administration.** The Secretary is responsible for administering this policy and shall report on compliance to the board annually.

## ARTICLE XII — WHISTLEBLOWER PROTECTION

**12.1 Reporting.** Any director, officer, volunteer, or employee who in good faith believes that the Corporation or any person acting on its behalf has engaged in a violation of law, of the Conflict of Interest Policy, of permit conditions, or of these Bylaws, may report the concern to the President, or, if the concern involves the President, to any other director.

**12.2 No Retaliation.** No person shall be subjected to retaliation, removal, discipline, or adverse treatment of any kind for making a good faith report under this Article, or for participating in an inquiry into such a report.

**12.3 Handling.** The board shall investigate each report promptly, shall document the investigation and its outcome, and shall keep the identity of the reporting person confidential to the extent consistent with a fair investigation.

## ARTICLE XIII — COMPENSATION OF OFFICERS AND KEY PERSONS

**13.1 Approval.** Any compensation paid by the Corporation to a director, officer, or key employee shall be approved in advance by a majority vote of directors who have no conflict of interest with respect to the arrangement.

**13.2 Comparability.** Before approving any such compensation, the board shall obtain and review data as to compensation paid by similarly situated organizations for functionally comparable positions.

**13.3 Documentation.** The basis for the determination, the comparability data relied upon, and the vote shall be recorded in the minutes contemporaneously with the decision.

**13.4 Recusal.** A person whose compensation is under consideration shall not be present for the deliberation or the vote, and shall not provide information to the board regarding their own compensation other than at the board's request.

## ARTICLE XIV — RECORDS AND REPORTS

The Corporation shall keep correct and complete books and records of account, minutes of the proceedings of the board and its committees, and a current roster of directors and officers. The Corporation shall make all required filings, including the IRS Form 990 series, the SCC annual registration, the VDACS charitable solicitation registration and renewal, and any annual report required by a permitting authority.

## ARTICLE XV — INDEMNIFICATION AND INSURANCE

**15.1 Indemnification.** To the fullest extent permitted by the Virginia Nonstock Corporation Act and the Articles of Incorporation, the Corporation shall indemnify each director and officer against liabilities and reasonable expenses incurred in connection with any proceeding arising from service to the Corporation, including any activity conducted on behalf of the Corporation under the authority of a permit or certification issued to that person by the Commonwealth of Virginia.

**15.2 Volunteers.** The board may extend indemnification to volunteers acting within the scope of duties assigned by the Corporation, on such terms as the board determines.

**15.3 Insurance.** The board shall obtain and maintain such insurance as it determines appropriate, and shall confirm in writing from the carrier that coverage extends to wildlife capture, handling, transport, and release conducted on behalf of the Corporation by individually permitted persons.

## ARTICLE XVI — FISCAL YEAR

The fiscal year of the Corporation shall be [January 1 through December 31].

## ARTICLE XVII — DISSOLUTION

Upon dissolution, the assets of the Corporation shall be distributed as provided in the Articles of Incorporation.

## ARTICLE XVIII — AMENDMENTS

These Bylaws may be amended or repealed, and new Bylaws adopted, by the affirmative vote of [two-thirds (2/3)] of the directors then in office at any meeting for which notice of the substance of the proposed amendment was given.
